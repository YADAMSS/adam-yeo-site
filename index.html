About

Dr Adam Yeo is an artist, designer and typographer, and Assistant Professor of Graphic Design at the University of Bondoukou, Côte d'Ivoire, where he leads the Arts, Design and Multimedia programme. His work moves between art, design and research, and focuses on African writing systems: how they are drawn, how they survive, and how they can live in digital form.

He is best known for his work on the Bété syllabary, an Indigenous script from Côte d'Ivoire, which he has helped document, digitise and bring to international attention. A script that cannot be typed on a phone or a computer is at risk of being forgotten; type design is one practical way to keep it in use.

He holds a doctorate in graphic design from Nanjing University of the Arts (China) and was a post-master researcher at the Atelier National de Recherche Typographique (ANRT) in Nancy, France.

Type design · Google Fonts · 2024

Matemasie

An ultra-bold display typeface with rounded edges and top-heavy letterforms, inspired by the Adinkra symbol Mate Masie — “what I hear, I keep” — a sign of wisdom, listening and knowledge. Free and open source.

View on Google Fonts
Type design · Unicode · since 2022

Bété typeface

A digital typeface for the Bété syllabary, created by Frédéric Bruly Bouabré. Developed at ANRT and with the Script Encoding Initiative (UC Berkeley), it supports the proposal to encode Bété in Unicode.
